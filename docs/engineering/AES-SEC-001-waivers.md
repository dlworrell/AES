## Waiver Requirements

Each approved waiver must identify:

- waiver ID and AES rule;
- exact file, line, or symbol;
- reason and safety invariant;
- compensating controls and test evidence;
- owner, review date, and expiration or recheck condition.

A waiver is local and specific. It does not amend the normative AES standard.
