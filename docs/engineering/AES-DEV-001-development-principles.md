## Local Expectations

AES documents should define standards that can be referenced by project repositories without duplicating their content.

A standard should identify its purpose, scope, status, enforcement direction, and relationship to other AES standards.

## Deviations

Changes that weaken or materially redefine an accepted engineering standard require an ADR or equivalent decision record.
